THE LITTLE DETAILS

Good questions, clear answers.

Is Emorva live yet?

Emorva is currently in development. You can join early access for updates; do not send money or sensitive profile details to anyone claiming to collect Emorva matching payments.

Can I use Emorva through Telegram?

Yes, a Telegram matchmaking assistant is part of the product plan. It will help adults navigate categories, search, request introductions and receive consent-based notifications.

Do I need to verify my identity?

Everyone must satisfy adult age-assurance requirements before adult matchmaking. Enhanced identity verification is a separate, optional trust level unless the service requires it for a particular feature.

What are connection fees for?

Published fees are for the platform's introduction service, never for a person's consent, date, sexual activity or guarantee of a meeting. Paid features will only launch with an approved payment provider.
